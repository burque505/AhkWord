# Themes

**View > Theme** colors the window: **Light** (the default), **Dark**, **Teal**, **Navy**, **Forest**, **Plum**, **Burgundy** or **Bronze**.

- The ribbon and the title bar take the theme's color at once. The darker themes have a ribbon of their own, with light icons.
- The rest of the window (the frame, the status bar, the panels, the dialogs) follows the theme from the next start of AhkWord.
- The page stays white in every theme, with black text, as it prints. So do the rulers' strips and the previews of a paragraph or a style.

The theme is kept in the settings (see [Settings and folders](../reference/settings.md)). A script can read or set it with `Rte.Theme`.
